How screenings work
  • Doors open fifteen minutes before the film. No entry after the first ten minutes.
  • Members get in free; guests pay 10 shekels at the door, cash only.
  • We vote on next month's list right after the credits. Stay for that.

About the club

Started in 2019 by three students who wanted a bigger screen than a laptop. We show one film a week during the semester, vote on next month's list at the end of each screening, and argue about it afterwards in the cafeteria.

Where we meet

Room 204 in the main building, except when the poster says otherwise. Doors open at 19:15.
