And the one place it is genuinely right. When you do not control the markup — a base rule for every <a> in prose, a ::selection colour, a third-party widget you cannot put a class on — there is nowhere to write a utility, and @apply is the correct tool. That is a handful of rules per project, in a base layer.

The rule to leave with: if you want @apply everywhere, what you actually want is a component. This week that is components.html. In week 9 it becomes a function, and then the problem stops existing rather than changing syntax.
